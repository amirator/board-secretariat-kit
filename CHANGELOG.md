# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (21 November 2026)

First release.

- Meeting input in YAML, registers in CSV; every input problem reported at once
- Agenda pack with timed agenda, member list, Hijri date and a matters-arising paper
- Draft minutes with attendance, quorum check, declarations of interest, numbered resolutions and actions
- Resolutions extract with vote counts and the decision rule used
- Resolutions register and action log in Excel with live formulas, drop-downs and overdue flags
- Matters-arising report for the next meeting
- Four editable Word templates (docxtpl), English with Arabic headings and names
- 44 tests; CI on Ubuntu and Windows, plus a LibreOffice job for PDFs and formula checks
