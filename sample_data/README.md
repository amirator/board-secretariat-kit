# Sample data

**Everything in this folder is SAMPLE / DEMO DATA.** "Demo Coastal Industries Co." does not exist. The directors,
managers, email addresses (`.example` domain), meetings, resolutions, actions and amounts were written for this demo.
They do not describe any real company, board, employer or person, and no real board papers were used.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `company.yaml` | Company name (English and Arabic), people, the Board and the Audit Committee, quorum and voting rules, weekend days, fonts |
| `meetings/AC-2026-04.yaml` | Audit Committee meeting of 11 Nov 2026: agenda, attendance and the secretary's record |
| `meetings/BOD-2026-04.yaml` | Board meeting of 18 Nov 2026, including a declared conflict, a split vote, a special resolution and a deferred item |
| `registers/resolutions_register.csv` | 20 earlier resolutions (BR/2026/001-012, ACR/2026/001-008) |
| `registers/action_log.csv` | 18 earlier actions (A-2026-001 to 018) in mixed states, some overdue |

The quorum and majority settings are examples. They are not a statement of what any law or articles of association
require; set them from your own governing documents.
